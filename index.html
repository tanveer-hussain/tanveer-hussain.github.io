Tanveer Hussain

Senior Lecturer in Computer Science, Edge Hill University · Director, KHAT-AI

Department of Computer Science

Edge Hill University

Ormskirk, Lancashire, UK

I am a Senior Lecturer in Computer Science at Edge Hill University, where I work on computer vision and artificial intelligence and teach machine learning at undergraduate and postgraduate level. I am also Director of KHAT-AI, which provides AI consultancy and training.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence from Sejong University, Seoul, South Korea (2022), and my BSc in Computer Science from Islamia College Peshawar (2017). Before joining Edge Hill, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Institute for Transport Studies, University of Leeds, working on perception for automated driving.

Research interests

  • Video understanding: single- and multi-view video summarisation, action and activity recognition
  • Saliency & segmentation: salient object detection (RGB and RGB-D), fire and smoke detection and segmentation
  • Scene understanding for autonomous driving
  • Efficient AI: deep models for resource-constrained, edge and IoT devices
  • Medical imaging: deep learning for clinical image analysis

My work has appeared in venues including CVPR Workshops, IEEE Transactions on Image Processing, IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics and Pattern Recognition. I am an IEEE member, serve on the editorial board of the Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Systems, and am a review editor for Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence.

I am happy to hear from prospective PhD/MSc students and collaborators interested in vision and AI.
