**KHAT-AI** is an AI consultancy and training company that I direct. We help universities, businesses and public-sector teams understand, adopt and build with artificial intelligence, bringing research-grade expertise to practical problems.

#### What we offer

- **AI training & upskilling** — from introductory "AI for everyone" sessions to hands-on, multi-day programmes in machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, NLP and generative AI.
- **Consultancy** — scoping AI opportunities, feasibility studies, model and data strategy, and technical review of AI projects.
- **Computer vision solutions** — video analytics, detection and segmentation, and efficient models for edge deployment.
- **Responsible AI** — guidance on evaluation, data governance and the responsible use of AI in practice.

#### Selected partners

Training and consultancy delivered for institutions including **KAUST / KAUST Academy** and colleges of the **University of Oxford**.
