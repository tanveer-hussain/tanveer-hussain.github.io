I am a Senior Lecturer in Computer Science at **Edge Hill University**, where I work on computer vision and artificial intelligence and teach machine learning at undergraduate and postgraduate level. I am also Director of **[KHAT-AI](/khat-ai/)**, which provides AI consultancy and training.

I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Software Convergence at **Sejong University**, Seoul, South Korea (2022), on deep models for multi-view video summarisation, and my BSc in Computer Science from Islamia College Peshawar (2017) as the batch Gold Medalist. Before joining Edge Hill, I was a Research Fellow in Computer Vision and Vehicle Automation at the **Institute for Transport Studies, University of Leeds**, working on vehicle automation, including the EU-funded Hi-Drive project, with industry partners such as Volkswagen, Audi, Ford, Volvo and BMW. I have also been a research intern at **MBZUAI**, Abu Dhabi, and Lab Coordinator of the Intelligent Media Laboratory at Sejong University.

#### Research interests

- **Video understanding:** single- and multi-view video summarisation, action and activity recognition
- **Saliency & segmentation:** salient object detection (RGB and RGB-D), fire and smoke detection and segmentation
- **Scene understanding** for autonomous driving
- **Vision-language models:** long-video understanding, adaptive test-time compute, zero-shot retrieval and reranking
- **Efficient AI:** deep models for resource-constrained, edge and IoT devices
- **Medical imaging:** deep learning for clinical image analysis

My work has appeared in venues including NeurIPS, CVPR, _IEEE Transactions on Image Processing_, _IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics_ and _Pattern Recognition_. I served as an **Area Chair for ACCV 2024**, review for venues including CVPR, NeurIPS, ECCV, IEEE TPAMI and IEEE TIP, serve on the editorial board of the _Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Systems_, and am a review editor for _Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence_. I am an IEEE member and a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA).

I am happy to hear from prospective **PhD/MSc students** and **collaborators** interested in vision and AI.
